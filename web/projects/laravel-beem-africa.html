What it does

A Laravel package that wraps Beem's APIs in one consistent interface: SMS, OTP, airtime top-ups, payment checkout, collections, contacts, Moja multi-channel messaging and international SMS.

Checkout can redirect to Beem's hosted page or embed in an iframe, and webhooks are handled through Laravel events. OTP send and verify flows come with detailed error codes, and Livewire and Vue components cover the common screens.

Highlights

  1. Redirect and iframe checkout with automatic webhook handling through Laravel events.
  2. OTP send and verify with 18 detailed error codes for precise handling.
  3. Airtime transfers across 40+ African networks, with balance and transaction status checks.
  4. Collections, contacts, Moja multi-channel messaging and international SMS.
  5. Pre-built Livewire and Vue/Inertia components for payment forms and verification.
  6. Optional transaction storage for payment records.

Install

composer require gowelle/laravel-beem-africa

Then add your Beem credentials to the package configuration.
