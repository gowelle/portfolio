What it does

Add the HasSku trait and Laravel e-commerce models get readable SKUs like TM-TSH-ABC12345-RED-LRG, generated from configurable prefixes, category accessors and variant rules.

Products and product variants are both supported out of the box, with per-model configuration for prefixes, separators and ULID length.

Highlights

  1. Readable SKUs: product prefix, category code, ULID fragment and variant suffixes.
  2. Add the HasSku trait; SKUs generate when models are created.
  3. Configurable prefixes, separators and ULID length.
  4. Per-model configuration for products and product variants.
  5. Supports Laravel 11, 12 and 13 on PHP 8.3+.
  6. Publishable configuration for app-specific rules.

Install

composer require gowelle/sku-generator

Publish the configuration with php artisan vendor:publish --tag=sku-generator-config.

Runs on

  • PHP 8.3+
  • Laravel 11, 12 or 13
