What it does

A type-safe Laravel wrapper for Flutterwave's v4 API. It covers the whole surface — charges, refunds, transfers, settlements, virtual accounts, wallets, orders, banks and mobile networks — behind PHP 8.3+ DTOs.

Retries, rate limiting, webhook verification and charge-session tracking are built in, so the payment plumbing stops being the fragile part of the project.

Highlights

  1. Complete v4 coverage: payments, refunds, transfers, settlements, virtual accounts, wallets, orders and more.
  2. Direct Charge orchestrator: customer, payment method and charge created in a single request.
  3. Exponential-backoff retries for 5xx errors, rate limits and timeouts.
  4. Configurable per-request rate limiting and secure webhook signature verification.
  5. Type-safe DTOs and detailed, categorised error handling.
  6. Livewire and Vue/Inertia components for payment forms, PIN/OTP entry and saved methods.
